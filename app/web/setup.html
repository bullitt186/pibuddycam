Prusa Connect

Register the camera with your printer in Prusa Connect.

No internet on this setup network, or the token is in the Prusa app on your phone? Choose Later. After the camera has joined your Wi-Fi, add the token in the console under Integrations.

  1. Open Prusa Connect and select your printer.
  2. Open Camera and add a camera.
  3. Copy the token it shows and paste it below.

The token stays on the camera and is never shown again.

Setup complete

The camera is joining your Wi-Fi and will appear in Prusa Connect within a minute or two.

  1. Reconnect this phone or laptop to your Wi-Fi.
  2. Open the local console at the camera's address. Your browser warns about the self-signed certificate once; that is expected.
  3. Sign in with the administrator password you just set.

Prusa Connect is not set up yet. Once you are back on your Wi-Fi, open the console, go to Integrations and paste the camera token from Prusa Connect. The camera then registers with Prusa Connect.

If the camera cannot join your Wi-Fi, the setup network PiBuddyCam-Setup comes back after a short while. Join it again and this page reopens so you can correct the details.
